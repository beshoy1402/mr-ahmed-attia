What you will master

The four SAT Reading
& Writing domains.

Organise your preparation around the official Reading and Writing domain framework. Each domain contains skills that can be practised and reviewed individually.

01

Information and Ideas

Read closely, identify central ideas, use evidence and interpret information.

02

Craft and Structure

Understand words in context, text purpose, structure and connections between ideas.

03

Expression of Ideas

Improve transitions, rhetorical synthesis and the logical flow of ideas.

04

Standard English Conventions

Strengthen grammar, punctuation, sentence boundaries and effective usage.
